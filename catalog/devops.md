## gstack — `garrytan/gstack`

> A full agent skill suite: ship/deploy workflows, QA, canary monitoring, security audits, iOS tooling, plan reviews, browser control, and more. Installs as its own suite rather than individual skills.

**Install:**

```bash
npx skills add garrytan/gstack
```

Browse: [github.com/garrytan/gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack)

Notable skills: `ship` · `land-and-deploy` · `canary` · `qa` · `qa-only` · `review` · `spec` · `investigate` · `cso` · `careful` · `guard` · `freeze`/`unfreeze` · `health` · `browse` · `scrape` · `plan-ceo-review` · `plan-eng-review` · `plan-design-review` · `ios-*` · `make-pdf` · `office-hours`

> gstack keeps its own state under `~/.gstack/` and upgrades via `gstack-upgrade`.
